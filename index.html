03 / How I approach a product problem

Start with the problem, not the feature.

This is the approach I would apply to a new product opportunity. It is a framework for my portfolio, not a claim that I have already led a commercial product launch.

01 — Understand

Identify the user, their task, and the friction in the existing process.

02 — Prioritize

Agree on the smallest useful scope and document trade-offs before development.

03 — Validate

Use feedback and a few meaningful measures to decide what should improve next.
